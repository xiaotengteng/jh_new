Business English Communication

Learn how to write effective emails, reports and presentations in English

Course Curriculum

Our Business English Communication course is specially designed for Indian professionals who need to communicate effectively in international business settings. This comprehensive 4-month program covers all aspects of written and verbal business communication, with a focus on practical applications relevant to today's corporate environment.

Module Breakdown

Module 1: Business Communication Essentials +

This module covers the fundamentals of business communication and common English errors made by Indian professionals.

  • Understanding different forms of business communication
  • Formal vs. informal language in business contexts
  • Common grammar and usage errors in business English
  • Cultural nuances in international business communication
  • Business vocabulary and terminology

Activities: Communication style analysis, error correction exercises, business vocabulary building

Module 2: Professional Email Communication +

Master the art of effective email communication in business settings.

  • Structuring professional emails
  • Subject lines that get responses
  • Email etiquette and protocols
  • Writing clear and concise messages
  • Email templates for different business situations
  • Follow-up emails and maintaining communication chains

Activities: Email drafting exercises, case studies, response analysis

Module 3: Business Reports and Documentation +

Learn to create professional business documents and reports.

  • Types of business reports and their structures
  • Executive summaries and abstracts
  • Data presentation and analysis in reports
  • Creating project proposals
  • Business case studies and white papers
  • Proofreading and editing techniques

Activities: Report writing practice, proposal drafting, peer review sessions

Module 4: Presentation Skills for Business +

Develop skills to create and deliver impactful business presentations.

  • Planning and structuring business presentations
  • Creating compelling slide decks
  • Data visualization techniques
  • Storytelling in business contexts
  • Handling Q&A sessions professionally
  • Virtual presentation skills

Activities: Presentation development, delivery practice, recorded feedback sessions

Module 5: Meeting Management +

Learn to participate in and lead effective business meetings.

  • Setting up meeting agendas
  • Running productive meetings
  • Taking comprehensive minutes
  • Participating effectively in discussions
  • Managing conflicts in meetings
  • Virtual meeting protocols

Activities: Meeting simulations, role-playing exercises, agenda creation

Module 6: Negotiation and Persuasive Communication +

Develop skills for effective negotiation and persuasion in business settings.

  • Persuasive language techniques
  • Negotiation strategies and vocabulary
  • Building compelling arguments
  • Handling objections professionally
  • Cross-cultural negotiation approaches
  • Win-win communication frameworks

Activities: Negotiation role plays, persuasive writing exercises, case study analyses

Module 7: Client Communication +

Master the art of effective client communication.

  • Understanding client needs and expectations
  • Managing client relationships
  • Handling difficult client situations
  • Creating client proposals and reports
  • Setting and managing expectations
  • Following up with clients

Activities: Client scenario role plays, proposal writing, feedback handling practice

Module 8: Capstone Project +

Apply all your learning in a comprehensive business communication project.

  • Developing a business proposal
  • Creating supporting documentation
  • Preparing and delivering a business presentation
  • Handling Q&A and feedback
  • Follow-up communication

Activities: Integrated business communication project with personalized feedback

Assessment and Certification

Your progress will be assessed throughout the course through:

  • Weekly writing assignments
  • Business document creation exercises
  • Presentation evaluations
  • Meeting participation and leadership
  • Final capstone project

Upon successful completion, you will receive a certificate of completion from JH KUMU EDTECH, with a detailed assessment of your business communication skills.
