Course Curriculum

Module Breakdown

Module 1: Foundation of Professional English +

This module establishes the core skills needed for professional English communication.

  • Assessment of current speaking abilities
  • Identifying common pronunciation challenges for Indian speakers
  • Introduction to stress, intonation, and rhythm in English
  • Building professional vocabulary
  • Understanding formal vs. informal language

Activities: Pronunciation drills, vocabulary building exercises, self-introduction in professional contexts

Module 2: Conversational Fluency +

Focus on developing natural conversation skills for workplace contexts.

  • Techniques for maintaining conversations
  • Active listening strategies
  • Asking effective questions
  • Small talk for networking events
  • Navigating difficult conversations

Activities: Role-playing workplace scenarios, networking simulations, impromptu speaking exercises

Module 3: Presentation Skills +

Learn to deliver impactful presentations with confidence.

  • Structure and organization of effective presentations
  • Visual aids and presentation tools
  • Audience engagement techniques
  • Handling Q&A sessions
  • Body language and stage presence

Activities: Mini-presentations with feedback, presentation structure exercises, impromptu speaking challenges

Module 4: Interview Excellence +

Master the art of job interviews in English.

  • Common interview questions and effective responses
  • Talking about your skills and experiences
  • Asking intelligent questions to interviewers
  • Virtual interview techniques
  • Salary negotiation language

Activities: Mock interviews, self-assessment videos, targeted feedback sessions

Module 5: Digital Communication +

Develop skills for effective online communication.

  • Virtual meeting etiquette and protocols
  • Online presentation delivery
  • Clear communication in video calls
  • Overcoming technical challenges
  • Building rapport in virtual settings

Activities: Virtual meeting simulations, online presentation practice, technical troubleshooting scenarios

Module 6: Industry-Specific Communication +

Tailored language practice for your professional field.

  • Industry-specific vocabulary and terminologies
  • Case studies from relevant sectors
  • Specialized communication scenarios
  • Networking within your industry
  • Professional growth language

Activities: Industry-specific role plays, specialized vocabulary building, case discussions

Assessment and Certification

Your progress will be assessed throughout the course through:

  • Regular speaking assessments
  • Recorded presentations
  • Mock interviews and feedback
  • Participation in group discussions
  • Final comprehensive assessment

Upon successful completion, you will receive a certificate of completion from JH KUMU EDTECH.
